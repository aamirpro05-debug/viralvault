What would happen if you could see the Milky Way from space?

From a dark location on Earth, the Milky Way appears as a bright band across the night sky. From above Earth's atmosphere, the view is different because there is no atmospheric haze between the observer and the stars.

The Milky Way is a galaxy containing an enormous number of stars, gas and dust. Our Solar System sits inside one of its spiral arms.

Space photography can reveal structures and colors that are difficult to see with the unaided eye.
