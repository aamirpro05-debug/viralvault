Why the ocean can look completely different in one place

Have you noticed that ocean water can look deep blue in one photo and green or turquoise in another? The color is influenced by sunlight, water depth, particles and microscopic life.

Deep, clear water often appears blue because water absorbs more of the longer red wavelengths of visible light. Near coasts, sand, sediment and organisms can scatter light and create greener or lighter colors.

That is why two beaches can look completely different even when they are on the same sea.
