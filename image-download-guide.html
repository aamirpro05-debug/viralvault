Image Download Guide

Simple tips for saving and managing images safely.

How to Download Images Safely

Images are useful for learning, projects and creative work. Always check whether you have permission to save and use an image.

1. Use Trusted Websites

Download images from trusted websites and use the official download option when available. Avoid suspicious pop-ups and unknown files.

2. Understand Image Formats

JPG is common for photographs, PNG is useful for transparent backgrounds, and WebP often provides smaller file sizes with good quality.

3. Check Image Quality

Choose an image resolution that suits your project. Larger images may look sharper but can use more storage and data.

4. Protect Your Device

Normal image files usually end in formats such as .jpg, .png or .webp. Be careful with unexpected downloads that ask you to install an app or provide personal information.

5. Respect Copyright

Use your own images, images you have permission to use, or images with suitable licences. Check the licence before reusing an image commercially.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which image format is common?
JPG, PNG and WebP are widely used.

Can I use every image I find online?
No. Some images are protected by copyright or have usage restrictions.

Are image download websites always safe?
No. Use trusted sources and avoid suspicious downloads.
