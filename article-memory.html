Why your brain sometimes remembers the wrong detail

Human memory is useful, but it is not a video recording. When we remember an event, the brain reconstructs details using stored information and context.

This means small details can sometimes change over time. Confidence and accuracy are not always the same thing.

The good news is that writing down important information soon after an event can help preserve more accurate details.
